I haven't read Charles Bukowski, don't plan on to in the near future. This is not a carbon copy of his writing or his style; it's my original reflection and attempt at experssing myself in writing. I'm just using this name because I think it's a cute name that fits what I want to write.

I'm writing this as a means of reflecting on my life and releasing the pressure that has been building in my mind. This is mainly for myself, and nothing more. It's not advice, and it's not any wisdom that anyone should take; it's just an attempt to understand my mind better.

Am I old? Most people would probably say not. Given how fast the internet goes these days, I might consider myself ancient relative to the internet. I'm calling myself old because I feel like the internet is what makes a big part of who I am. The internet raised me, it defines my professional and personal personality, and it is a dimension of my existence. Am I old in real life? No, but that's not the point. The point of me writing this is the anonymous nature, to hopefully reflect on my true self, not the one that is brought up by my physical status. I am going to be as honest as I want to be, and I hope I don’t get blacklisted by Palantir by this.

Even though I haven't read Bukowski, there is a quote by him that did inspire me to create this:

"Can you remember who you were, before the world told you who you should be?”

That's what I want to discover throughout me writing this.
